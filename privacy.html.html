Final Fantasy Dex — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Final Fantasy Dex ("FFDex", "the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and handles information when users interact with the Final Fantasy Dex Discord bot.

Final Fantasy Dex is a character-collecting and trading Discord bot that allows users to collect, trade, and manage virtual Final Fantasy characters.

By using Final Fantasy Dex, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Final Fantasy Dex may collect and store certain information necessary to provide its features and maintain its services.

The information collected may include the following:

1.1 Discord Account Information

When interacting with Final Fantasy Dex, we may collect:

  • Discord User IDs.
  • Discord Server IDs.
  • Discord usernames or other identifying information provided through Discord.
  • Information necessary to identify users when processing bot commands.

We primarily use Discord IDs to identify accounts and associate game progress with the appropriate users.

We do not request or collect Discord account passwords.

1.2 Game and Collection Data

To provide the bot's gameplay features, we may collect and store:

  • Collected characters and inventory information.
  • Character rarities and quantities.
  • Collection statistics and milestones.
  • Achievement progress.
  • Leaderboard statistics.
  • In-game currency or other game-related progression, if applicable.

This information is necessary to maintain user collections and provide the bot's gameplay functionality.

1.3 Trading and Gifting Information

When users participate in trades or gifts, we may store information such as:

  • Participating Discord User IDs.
  • Characters involved in the transaction.
  • Trade and gifting records.
  • Transaction timestamps.
  • Information necessary to investigate reported trading disputes or suspicious activity.

This information helps maintain accurate transaction records and investigate potential exploitation or violations of our Terms of Service.

1.4 Command and Operational Information

We may collect limited operational information associated with bot usage, including:

  • Commands used.
  • Command timestamps.
  • Error reports and diagnostic information.
  • Information necessary to identify bugs, exploits, or technical issues.

This information may be used to maintain the reliability and functionality of Final Fantasy Dex.

2. How We Use Collected Information

Information collected by Final Fantasy Dex may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing and maintaining bot functionality.
  • Managing user collections and inventories.
  • Processing character encounters, catches, trades, and gifts.
  • Maintaining achievements and leaderboards.
  • Identifying users and processing commands.
  • Detecting cheating, exploitation, and unauthorized automation.
  • Investigating reports and enforcing our Terms of Service.
  • Diagnosing technical problems and improving bot performance.
  • Maintaining the security and integrity of the service.

We do not intentionally collect information unrelated to operating, maintaining, or protecting Final Fantasy Dex.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

Final Fantasy Dex does not intentionally sell users' personal information.

We do not publicly disclose private user information unless necessary for operating the service, enforcing our rules, or complying with applicable legal obligations.

Information may be disclosed in the following circumstances:

3.1 Authorized Staff

Authorized members of the Final Fantasy Dex Team may access relevant user and game information when necessary to:

  • Investigate reports.
  • Review suspicious activity.
  • Resolve technical issues.
  • Investigate exploits or cheating.
  • Enforce our Terms of Service.

Staff access should be limited to information reasonably necessary for these responsibilities.

3.2 Service Providers

Final Fantasy Dex may rely on third-party services to operate the bot, such as hosting providers, database providers, or other technical services.

These providers may process or store information necessary to operate the service, subject to their respective policies and applicable arrangements.

3.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose relevant information if required to do so by applicable law, valid legal requests, or legal proceedings.

4. Data Storage and Security

Final Fantasy Dex stores information necessary to maintain user accounts, collections, and gameplay progress.

We take reasonable steps to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no online service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We cannot guarantee that information will never be lost, accessed without authorization, or affected by technical failures.

Users should never share passwords, financial information, home addresses, or other sensitive personal information through the bot.

5. Data Retention

Final Fantasy Dex may retain information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide the bot's functionality.
  • Maintain user collections and gameplay progress.
  • Preserve relevant trade and transaction records.
  • Investigate violations of our Terms of Service.
  • Maintain operational records and service integrity.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We reserve the right to delete outdated, unnecessary, or otherwise unwanted information when appropriate.

We do not guarantee permanent storage of user data.

6. Data Deletion Requests

Users who wish to request the deletion of information associated with their Final Fantasy Dex account may contact the Final Fantasy Dex Team through the official Discord server.

Deletion requests may be reviewed by authorized staff.

When submitting a request, users may be asked to provide their Discord User ID to verify account ownership.

Please be aware that:

  • Certain information may need to be retained for security, moderation, or legal purposes.
  • Deleting game data may result in the permanent loss of collections, achievements, and gameplay progress.
  • Some information may remain temporarily in backups or technical records.
  • We cannot guarantee the immediate deletion of all information from every system.

The Final Fantasy Dex Team reserves the right to review deletion requests in accordance with applicable laws and operational requirements.

7. Discord and Third-Party Services

Final Fantasy Dex operates through Discord and relies on Discord's platform and infrastructure to provide its functionality.

Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, policies, or security of Discord or other third-party services.

For additional information, please refer to Discord's official Privacy Policy:

https://discord.com/privacy

8. Children's Privacy

Final Fantasy Dex is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information from users.

If you believe that someone has provided personal information to Final Fantasy Dex inappropriately, please contact our team through the official Discord server.

9. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for protecting their own Discord accounts and personal information.

We strongly encourage users to:

  • Never share Discord passwords or authentication credentials.
  • Avoid submitting sensitive personal information through bot commands.
  • Be cautious when interacting with unfamiliar users.
  • Report suspected security vulnerabilities or unauthorized access.
  • Review Discord's own privacy and security settings.

Final Fantasy Dex staff will never legitimately require your Discord password.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Final Fantasy Dex Team reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time.

Changes may be announced through the official Final Fantasy Dex Discord server or other official communication channels.

Users are responsible for reviewing this policy periodically.

Continued use of Final Fantasy Dex after changes take effect constitutes acknowledgment of the updated Privacy Policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

The most recent revision date will be displayed at the beginning of this document.
